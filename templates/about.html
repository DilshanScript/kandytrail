About KandyTrail

Plan a one-day trip around Kandy in a few taps. Find temples, gardens, waterfalls, heritage sites and places to eat, then get a day plan with times and directions.

How it works

  1. 1
    Explore

    Browse places by category, search by name and see opening hours, fees, tips and a map.

  2. 2
    Plan your day

    Answer a few questions and let KandyTrail plan it, or pick each place yourself with live distance and drive time.

  3. 3
    Go

    Open the whole trip in Google Maps, print it, or sign in to save and share it with other travellers.

About this project

KandyTrail was built as an individual project for ITE2953 Programming Group Project, Bachelor of Information Technology, University of Moratuwa. It uses Python Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Google Maps for maps and drive times and Open-Meteo for the weather.
