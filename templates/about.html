Why KandyTrail

Kandy has temples, gardens, waterfalls, heritage sites and local food spread across the hills around the city. Many visitors only have one day, and a lot of it is lost driving back and forth, reaching a place after it closes or missing a great spot that was only a few minutes away.

KandyTrail brings everything you need into one place: the places worth visiting, their opening hours and entry fees, real drive times between them and the weather for the day. Whether you are a tourist, a local family or a group of friends, you can turn a free day into a well planned trip.

How it helps you

Save time

Places are put in an order that keeps driving short, so you spend more of the day visiting.

Arrive when it is open

Opening hours and closed days are checked for the date you pick, with a warning if a visit runs late.

Know before you go

See fees, visit times, travel tips, reviews and the weather before you leave home.

Plan your way

Get a ready-made plan or build your own, then save it, share it or open it in Google Maps.
